Our Story

A Kitchen Filled With Love & Tradition

Homestead Roots is a homemade food cloud kitchen by VANI born from the belief that the best food comes from the heart. We bring you authentic, fresh vegetarian meals prepared with quality ingredients and a genuine homely touch.

Every dish we prepare carries the warmth of home cooking — because we believe food should nourish not just your body, but your soul. From comforting breakfasts to celebratory cakes and catering spreads, everything is made fresh, every single day.

❤️ “Good food, happy mood. We prepare every meal with the same warmth and pure ingredients we serve our own family.”
